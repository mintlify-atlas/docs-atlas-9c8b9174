---
title: "Logging"
description: "Control log output for the exporter and probe-level logs independently."
---

The Blackbox Exporter maintains two fully independent structured loggers.

| Logger | Flag | Default level | Scope |
|--------|------|---------------|-------|
| Application logger | `--log.level` | `info` | Startup, config reload, HTTP server, internal errors |
| Probe/scrape logger | `--log.prober` | `info` | HTTP, TCP, ICMP, DNS, gRPC, Unix, WebSocket probe execution |

Valid levels for both flags are `debug`, `info`, `warn`, and `error`.

## Log format

Both loggers emit structured log lines with `time`, `level`, `source`, and `msg` fields, along with additional key-value attributes depending on the event:

```
time=2025-09-09T00:58:02.557-04:00 level=INFO source=main.go:95 msg="Starting blackbox_exporter" version="..."
```

## Probe log levels

The `--log.prober` flag controls how much detail appears in probe logs. Probe logs are written to stderr and are also captured per-probe for retrieval via `?debug=true`.

<Note>
  Probe logs always appear in the `?debug=true` output regardless of `--log.prober` level, because the scrape logger writes to an internal buffer independently of the stderr log level filter.
</Note>
